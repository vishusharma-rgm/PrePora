# Delivery checklist

## Implemented in this slice

- Responsive landing page and authenticated-style app shell.
- Light/dark theme toggle.
- Dashboard with focus plan, activity, subject pulse and countdown.
- Practice lab and custom test builder.
- Objective exam interface with answer selection and result summary.
- Analytics, chapter mastery, subject breakdown and insight surfaces.
- Study planner with interactive completion state.
- Mistake notebook with tutor/retry actions.
- Tutor conversation shell with context, prompts and honest AI limitation note.
- FastAPI health, exam catalog, dashboard and test-generation contracts.

## Not yet production-verified

- Persistent authentication, PostgreSQL migrations and user isolation.
- Server-authoritative timer/submission persistence.
- Licensed question-bank ingestion and admin moderation.
- Live AI provider, RAG and realtime voice credentials.
- Playwright end-to-end suite and deployment smoke tests.

These items are deliberately called out rather than represented by fake success states.
